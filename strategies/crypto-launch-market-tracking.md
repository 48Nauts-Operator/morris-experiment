# Crypto Launch Market Tracking Strategy

## Market Intelligence Objectives
1. Identify imminent crypto project launches
2. Detect high-potential security audit opportunities
3. Create targeted, time-sensitive outreach mechanism

## Launch Tracking Dimensions
- Project Name
- Launch Date
- Blockchain Technology
- Funding Stage
- Potential Security Vulnerabilities

## Opportunity Scoring Criteria
### High-Risk Indicators
- First-time blockchain implementation
- Complex smart contract architecture
- DeFi/Yield protocols
- Cross-chain functionality
- Substantial initial funding

## Audit Offering Tiers
1. **Quick Scan** (50 CHF)
   - Rapid preliminary vulnerability assessment
   - 24-hour turnaround
   - Basic smart contract review

2. **Comprehensive Audit** (500 CHF)
   - Full security infrastructure analysis
   - Detailed vulnerability report
   - Mitigation strategy development
   - 48-hour in-depth review

## Outreach Mechanism
- Automated launch tracking
- Personalized security proposal
- Urgent risk communication
- Limited-time introductory offer

## Target Segments
- ICO/Token Launch Projects
- New DeFi Protocols
- Layer 2 Solutions
- Cross-Chain Platforms
- Emerging Blockchain Technologies

## Conversion Strategy
- Create sense of urgent risk mitigation
- Highlight potential financial losses
- Offer immediate, actionable insights
- Time-sensitive pricing

## Metrics and Goals
- Track 250+ upcoming launches
- Identify 50+ high-potential targets
- Aim for 10 paid security engagements
- Minimum revenue target: 5,000 CHF
